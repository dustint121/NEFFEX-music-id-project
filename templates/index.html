    About NEFFEX

    NEFFEX is an independent music project from Orange County, California, started in 2014 by vocalist Bryce Savage and producer Cameron Wales. Bryce Savage carries it on today.

    The sound is a high-energy mix of hip-hop, alternative rock and EDM with pop hooks. Most songs are motivational: they are about pushing through hard times, betting on yourself and chasing your goals, which is why they show up so often in workout, gaming and sports videos.

    Most of the catalog is released as copyright-free music, so creators can put it in their videos and streams, including monetized ones, without copyright claims. That has made NEFFEX a favorite soundtrack for creators everywhere, including me: I use these songs in my own music videos, and this app can tell you which one is playing.
